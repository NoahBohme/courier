Webhook URL

Any method, any sub-path (…/stripe, …/github?x=1). Requests show up here live. To receive webhooks from the internet, expose Courier with a tunnel, e.g. cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:7878.

Import cURL

Paste a curl command. Method, URL, params, headers, auth and body are filled in for you.

Variables

Use {{name}} in the URL, headers or body — e.g. {{baseUrl}}/users or Bearer {{token}}. Values are swapped in when you send.
